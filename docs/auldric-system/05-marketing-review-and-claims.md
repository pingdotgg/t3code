# Marketing review and claims

## Review and provenance target

Every canonical Marketing decision, artifact revision, review transition, and approval records an
immutable actor reference, role, timestamp, object version, source lineage, rationale, and before/
after state. Author, reviewer, and approver remain distinct. Revocation prevents future action but
does not rewrite policy-safe historical provenance. Missing identity or authorization cannot produce
an unattributed review or approval.

T3 continues to own ordinary message authorship, Dev commands, tool approvals, and runtime receipts.
Auldric does not create a parallel generic command, identity, or execution audit system.

## Claims rule

Today, public copy must not claim that this repository ships an Auldric Marketing workspace. After
implementation, a material claim is publishable only when the exact user path has current proof and
the claim names its boundary accurately.

Never claim unsupported autonomy, unlimited model usage, guaranteed growth or revenue, broad
connector action coverage, complete closed-loop measurement, instant strategy without sources, or
saved/reviewed state that lacks canonical read-back.

Before publication, verify:

- the exact capability is reachable by a new authorized user;
- source, assumption, review, and failure states are visible;
- persistence and organization isolation are proved where claimed;
- provider, access, connector, price, distribution, and availability language matches current fact;
- the claim does not turn planned work or donor evidence into product truth.

Issue #3 owns identity decisions and #27 owns the public surface. This file supplies claims safety,
not branding or distribution authority.
