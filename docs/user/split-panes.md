# Split panes

Show up to six conversations side by side and work in any of them without switching threads.

## Open a split

- Drag a thread from the sidebar to the **New pane** strip that appears on the right edge of the window.
- Or right-click a thread and choose **Open in split pane**.

Drag a thread onto an existing pane to swap what that pane shows. A thread that is already open moves to the pane you drop on instead of opening twice.

## Work in panes

The pane you click is the active one. The sidebar highlights its thread, the URL follows it, and keyboard shortcuts and the composer act on it. Right panels such as diffs and files open as a sheet over their pane.

Drag the grip in a pane's header onto another pane to swap the two conversations.

Drag the divider between panes to resize them. Dividers snap to equal sizes and to line up with dividers in neighboring rows. Hold Alt to resize freely, or double-click a divider to make every pane equal again. Focus a divider and use the arrow keys to nudge it.

Hover a pane and use the close button in its header to remove it. Close down to one pane to return to the normal single view. Your panes come back when you restart.

Split panes need a window at least as wide as a tablet. Narrower windows show one thread at a time and keep your panes for when the window is wide again.
