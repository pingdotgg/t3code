# Mobile notifications

Push notifications, Live Activities, Android Live Updates, and the **Agent Activity** widget are not available. They were delivered through T3 Connect, which has been removed, and a direct or Tailscale connection cannot wake a phone.

To see what your agents are doing, open T3 Code on your phone. It connects to your environments and shows each thread's current state, including work that finished, failed, or is waiting for your approval or input.
