# Use passkeys in the browser

On the Linux desktop app, choose a website's passkey sign-in option, then scan the QR code with
the phone or tablet that holds your passkey. Keep both devices nearby with Bluetooth enabled
and an internet connection, then approve the request on your phone.

You can also create a passkey on your phone when a website offers registration. Passkeys remain
on your phone or in its password manager. Passkey autofill in sign-in fields is not supported;
use the website's explicit passkey button.

Choose **Use a security key** to use a USB authenticator instead. Keys that require entering a
PIN may not work on Linux.
