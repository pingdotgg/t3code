T3 Code is an open-source control plane for coding agents, with desktop, web, and mobile clients. This library explains how to install it, connect your agents, and manage their work across your computers.

## Choose your starting point

Follow [Get started](/learn/start) to choose an installer, confirm the official download, and complete setup. If T3 Code is already running, use [Coding agents](/learn/providers) to connect the provider you want. Provider installation and authentication belong to the computer running your agents.

Use [Threads and messages](/learn/threads) to organize conversations and supply context. Open [Remote and mobile](/learn/remote) when you want another computer or your phone to control that work.

## Learn the parts of your workflow

Read [Permissions and privacy](/learn/safety) before choosing how much agents can do. Use [Git, terminal, and previews](/learn/workspace) when you need to inspect changes and work with their results. Visit [Settings and customization](/learn/customize) to adjust T3 Code for your projects.

Explore [Ideas behind T3 Code](/learn/concepts) for the distinction between models, harnesses, and a control plane. Watch [Theo on T3 Code](/learn/videos) for demonstrations with timestamped source videos. Read [Open source](/learn/open-source) for the license, architecture, and contribution paths.

## Next steps

- Get an installer from the [official download page](/download), then follow [Get started](/learn/start).
- Choose a provider through the [coding agent guides](/learn/providers) and review [permissions and privacy](/learn/safety).
