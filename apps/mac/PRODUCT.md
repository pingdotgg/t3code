# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers supervising coding agents across local and remote Macs. They need to start, monitor, review, and recover concurrent coding sessions without leaving the native app.

## Product Purpose

SurgeCode is a native Apple client for coding agents. It keeps multi-provider sessions, project state, diffs, approvals, and remote-device workflows predictable under load and during reconnects or partial failures.

## Brand Personality

Concise, calm, alpine. The interface should feel capable and native, keeping attention on the developer's work rather than on its own chrome.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic browser dashboard, a decorative AI chat wrapper, or a control-dense operations console. Alpine imagery belongs on designated brand and scenery surfaces, not as decoration in sidebars, toolbars, sheets, or Settings.

## Design Principles

- Make the primary session workflow obvious and consistent across local and remote devices.
- Prefer native, familiar controls that disappear into the task.
- Preserve context and predictable state through reconnects, restarts, and partial streams.
- Keep high-frequency controls compact while making their availability and failure states explicit.
- Let scenery provide identity without competing with code, conversation, or review content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native SwiftUI semantics, labels, focus behavior, and keyboard conventions. Respect macOS Reduce Motion, never rely on color alone for status, and keep text and controls legible in the app's dark appearance.
